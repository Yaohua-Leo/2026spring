## Def module

Let $R$ be a ring with $1, M$ be an abelian group．We say that $M$ is a left $R$－module if $R$＂left acts＂ on $M$ ：

$$
R \times M \rightarrow M ;(r, m) \mapsto r m
$$

such that

$$
\begin{aligned}
& r(m+n)=r m+r n \\
& (r+s) m=r m+s m \\
& (r s) m=r(s m)
\end{aligned} \quad \text { for any } r, s \in R .
$$

right $R$－module：$(r, m) \mapsto m r$
$m(r s)=(m r) s$ left－module $=$ right module.
